Vision and roadmap

The purchase request system is the first module of a larger internal operations platform for the group.

  1. Internal purchase requestsThis project: request, stock check, procurement approval, signed PDF.
  2. Store and inventoryStock per store, receipts, issues to projects. Fills "available in store" automatically.
  3. ProcurementSuppliers, quotations, purchase orders and invoices, checked against internal standards.
What this means for the design now
  • A shared core that later modules reuse: companies, users and roles, projects and cost centres, items and units, approvals and signing, PDF and sealing, notifications, audit trail.
  • The PR module sits on top of that core, self-contained.
  • Hosting starts on Railway and may move to AWS, pay per use. Only portable building blocks (containers, PostgreSQL, S3-compatible file storage, Microsoft Graph or SMTP for email), so the move is a deployment change, not a rewrite.
